# Shoulder Isometrics (6-Way)

## Basic info

- Name: Shoulder Isometrics (6-Way)
- Category: Isometric / Rotator Cuff Activation
- Exercise goal: Maintain muscle tone and strengthen the rotator cuff without joint movement.
- Source: YouTube (Shoulder Isometrics)
- Reference video/link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8Owu4S_69RY
- Description of ideal performance: Static pressure against a wall or doorframe in 6 directions (Flexion, Extension, Abduction, Adduction, Internal/External Rotation) with a 90-degree elbow bend.

## Importance

Exercise weight in the current cycle:

- `4` — key (crucial for maintaining muscle during early recovery phases)

Why it matters:
- Safely activates the rotator cuff when movement is restricted or painful.
- Prevents muscle atrophy without stressing the joint capsule or surgical repairs.

## Performance recommendations

- Frequency: 1-2 times daily
- Sets: 1 per direction
- Reps: 10 holds per direction
- Time: 5–10 seconds hold
- Tempo: Isometric (static)
- Range of motion: None (static)
- Pauses: Minimal between directions

## What to track

- pain before/after;
- percentage of max force applied (20% to 80%);
- stability of the torso;
- breath holding (avoid it);
- subjective difficulty.

## Common errors

- **Too much force too fast:** Not ramping up gradually.
- **Joint movement:** Turning it into an isotonic exercise by moving the arm.
- **Compensating with torso:** Leaning into the wall instead of using the shoulder.
- **Holding breath:** Forgetting to breathe during the hold.

## Restrictions

What is forbidden or undesirable:

- Do not push to 100% force if it causes pain.
- No jerky movements.

## Conditions for adding to a cycle

- Minimum recovery stage: Early post-op / early injury phase.
- Whether protocol approval/prescription is required: Yes.
- Which conditions must be met: Clearance for isometric activation.
