## Exercise idea

The user lies on their back, holds a stick/dowel with both hands, and slowly takes the arms overhead as far as is comfortable. The healthy/less-restricted side assists the movement.

## Reference performance

- Lie on your back.
- Knees bent.
- Hold the dowel with both hands.
- Slowly bring the arms overhead.
- Use the other arm's assistance as needed.
- Move only within a comfortable range.
- Return back under control.

## What to track

- pain before/after;
- approximate shoulder flexion range;
- smoothness of movement;
- whether there are jerks;
- whether there's compensation through the torso/ribs/neck;
- whether the movement is becoming more confident;
- whether the range is increasing on trend.

## Common errors

- pushing through pain;
- letting the arms drop back sharply;
- arching the lower back instead of moving from the shoulder;
- shrugging the shoulders toward the ears;
- chasing range instead of control.
