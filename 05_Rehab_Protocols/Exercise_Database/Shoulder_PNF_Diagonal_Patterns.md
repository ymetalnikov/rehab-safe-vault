# Shoulder PNF Diagonal Patterns (D1/D2)

## Goal

Improve neuromuscular control, proprioception, and functional stability of the shoulder. These patterns simulate real-world movements (like reaching or climbing) by combining rotation, abduction/adduction, and flexion/extension.

## Cues

- **D1 "Seatbelt":** Move from your hip to the opposite ear. Rotate your palm toward your face at the top.
- **D2 "The Sword":** Move from the opposite hip up and out. Rotate your thumb up and back (like drawing a sword).
- **Lead with Rotation:** Start the movement by rotating your hand/wrist.
- **Eyes on Hand:** Follow your hand with your eyes to improve coordination and range of motion.
- **Smooth Diagonal:** Ensure the path is a straight diagonal line, not a curve.

## Ideal performance

Perform these movements standing or sitting with a straight back.

1. **D1 Pattern:** Reach across to the opposite hip (thumb down). Pull up and across to the same-side ear (thumb up/palm toward face).
2. **D2 Pattern:** Reach across to the opposite hip (thumb down). Pull up and away to the same side (thumb up and back).

## Performance recommendations

- Frequency: 3-4 times per week
- Sets: 2-3 per pattern
- Reps: 10-12 per side
- Time: N/A
- Tempo: Controlled and deliberate
- Range of motion: Full comfortable range
- Pauses: 30-60 seconds

## What to track

- pain before/after
- smoothness of the diagonal path
- coordination of the rotation
- subjective difficulty
- any "clicks" or "catches" in the joint

## Common errors

- **Lack of Rotation:** Moving the arm without turning the palm/thumb.
- **Breaking the Diagonal:** Turning the movement into a side raise or front raise.
- **Speeding:** Moving too fast to feel the control.
- **Shrugging:** Using the upper trap to lift the arm.

## Restrictions

- Avoid if it causes sharp pinching in the joint.
- Use caution if you have active impingement symptoms.

## Conditions for adding to a cycle

- Minimum recovery stage: Mid-stage.
- Required equipment: None.
- Whether protocol approval/prescription is required: Recommended.
- Other conditions: Basic pain-free active ROM in all planes.
