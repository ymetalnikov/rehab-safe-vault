# Shoulder Prone I-Y-T

## Goal

Scapular stabilization and strengthening of the muscles surrounding the shoulder blade (lower/mid trapezius, rhomboids, posterior deltoids) to improve posture and shoulder joint health.

## Cues

- **Thumbs Up:** Keep thumbs pointing toward the ceiling (external rotation) to clear the joint space.
- **Scapular Retraction:** Initiate every movement by squeezing the shoulder blades together and down ("toward back pockets").
- **Neutral Neck:** Keep your gaze toward the floor; maintain a neutral spine to avoid tension.
- **Controlled Tempo:** Perform slow lifts with a 1–2 second hold at the top.

## Ideal performance

The exercise is performed lying face down (prone) on the floor or a Gym Ball. The movement is initiated by the shoulder blades.

1. **"I" Position:** Arms by your sides. Lift them straight back toward your hips, squeezing the shoulder blades together.
2. **"Y" Position:** Arms extended forward at a 45-degree angle (Y-shape). Lift with thumbs pointing up.
3. **"T" Position:** Arms spread straight out to the sides (T-shape). Lift with thumbs pointing up, maximizing scapular squeeze.

## Performance recommendations

- Frequency: 2-3 times per week
- Sets: 2-3
- Reps: 10-12 per direction (30-36 total reps per set)
- Time: N/A
- Tempo: Controlled (2 sec up, 1-2 sec hold, 2 sec down)
- Range of motion: Full comfortable lift without torso compensation.
- Pauses: 60 seconds between sets

## What to track

- pain before/after
- range of motion
- smoothness
- torso compensation (lifting chest off the ball/floor using the lower back)
- scapular compensation (shrugging shoulders toward ears)
- neck/trapezius compensation
- subjective difficulty

## Common errors

- **Shrugging:** Lifting shoulders toward the ears instead of retracting the shoulder blades.
- **Neck Strain:** Attempting to look forward or craning the neck.
- **Torso Compensation:** Using the lower back muscles to help lift the arms.
- **Thumb Position:** Turning thumbs inward (internal rotation), which increases the risk of impingement.

## Restrictions

- Avoid if sharp joint pain occurs during the movement.
- Do not perform if unable to maintain scapular control (shoulders "drifting" upward).

## Conditions for adding to a cycle

- Minimum recovery stage: Mid-stage (active motion cleared).
- Required equipment: Gym Ball or a flat surface.
- Whether protocol approval/prescription is required: Yes.
- Other conditions: Ability to perform active scapular retraction without pain.
