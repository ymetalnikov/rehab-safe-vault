# Shoulder Theraband 3-Way

## Basic info

- Name: Shoulder Theraband 3-Way
- Category: Strengthening / Multi-planar Stability
- Exercise goal: Strengthen the shoulder musculature and improve stability across three planes of motion.
- Source: YouTube (Shoulder Theraband Flexion, Abduction, and Scaption)
- Reference video/link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=76OUuWLAjQs
- Description of ideal performance: Standing upright, stepping on a resistance band, and lifting the straight arm to 90 degrees in three directions: front (flexion), side (abduction), and diagonal (scaption).

## Importance

Exercise weight in the current cycle:

- `4` — key (essential for functional shoulder strength)

Why it matters:
- Targets all heads of the deltoid and the supraspinatus.
- Teaches proper scapular-humeral rhythm under resistance.

## Equipment Required

- **Resistance Band:** [[02_Conventions/Equipment_Inventory#Resistance_Bands|Level 1-3]] (Yellow to Green).
- **Wall / Doorframe:** (Optional for balance).

## Performance recommendations

- Frequency: 2-3 times per week
- Sets: 2-3
- Reps: 10-12 per direction (30-36 total per set)
- Time: N/A
- Tempo: Controlled (2 sec up, 2 sec down)
- Range of motion: Up to 90 degrees (shoulder height)
- Pauses: 60 seconds between sets

## What to track

- pain before/after;
- thumb position (always pointing up);
- scapular shrugging (avoid it);
- torso leaning (keep core engaged);
- subjective difficulty.

## Common errors

- **Shrugging:** Lifting the shoulder toward the ear.
- **Leaning:** Tilting the body to help lift the arm.
- **Thumb Position:** Letting the thumb turn down or inward (increases impingement risk).
- **Over-lifting:** Going above 90 degrees without perfect control.

## Restrictions

What is forbidden or undesirable:
- Do not use a band that forces you to compromise form or shrug.
- Avoid if sharp joint pain occurs at the 90-degree mark.

## Conditions for adding to a cycle

- Minimum recovery stage: Mid-stage (active motion to 90 degrees cleared).
- Whether protocol approval/prescription is required: Yes.
- Which conditions must be met: Pain-free active-assisted range of motion.
