# Shoulder Theraband ER/IR

## Basic info

- Name: Shoulder Theraband ER/IR
- Category: Strengthening / Rotator Cuff Stability
- Exercise goal: Strengthen the rotator cuff muscles (Infraspinatus, Teres Minor, Subscapularis) to improve joint stability.
- Source: YouTube (Shoulder Theraband External and Internal Rotation)
- Reference video/link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f6XcNrDb200
- Description of ideal performance: Standing with a towel pinned between the elbow and side, rotating the forearm outward (ER) or inward (IR) against the resistance of a band while maintaining a 90-degree elbow bend and neutral wrist.

## Importance

Exercise weight in the current cycle:

- `5` — main exercise of the cycle (Gold standard for rotator cuff rehab)

Why it matters:
- Directly targets the stabilizers of the shoulder joint.
- Helps prevent impingement by improving the downward pull of the humeral head during elevation.

## Performance recommendations

- Frequency: 3-4 times per week
- Sets: 2-3 per direction
- Reps: 10-15
- Time: N/A
- Tempo: Slow and controlled (2 sec out, 2 sec back)
- Range of motion: Pain-free range (usually ~30-45 degrees for ER)
- Pauses: 30-60 seconds

## What to track

- pain before/after;
- ability to keep the towel pinned;
- wrist neutrality;
- scapular position (staying down and back);
- subjective difficulty.

## Common errors

- **Elbow Drift:** Allowing the elbow to move away from the ribs (losing the towel).
- **Wrist Cheating:** Bending the wrist to gain extra range.
- **Using Momentum:** Moving too fast or using the torso to pull the band.
- **Shrugging:** Lifting the shoulder toward the ear.

## Restrictions

What is forbidden or undesirable:
- Do not use a band that is too heavy, causing technique breakdown.
- Avoid range of motion that causes sharp or "pinching" pain.

## Conditions for adding to a cycle

- Minimum recovery stage: Mid-stage (once active resistance is cleared).
- Whether protocol approval/prescription is required: Yes.
- Which conditions must be met: Pain-free active motion in the required range.
