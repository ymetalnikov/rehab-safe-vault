# Shoulder Supine Serratus Punch

## Goal

Strengthen the serratus anterior muscle and improve scapular stabilization. This is foundational for preventing scapular winging and ensuring healthy shoulder mechanics during pushing or overhead movements.

## Cues

- **Locked Elbow:** Keep your arm perfectly straight throughout the movement; do not bend the elbow.
- **Punch the Ceiling:** Reach your hand as high as possible by moving only the shoulder blade.
- **Controlled Return:** Lower the shoulder back to the floor slowly and with control.
- **Vertical Alignment:** Keep the arm pointed directly at the ceiling; avoid letting it drift toward your head or feet.

## Ideal performance

Perform this exercise lying flat on your back (supine) on a firm surface.

1. **Starting Position:** Lie on your back with one arm extended straight up toward the ceiling.
2. **Action:** Without bending your elbow, "punch" your hand further toward the ceiling by lifting your shoulder blade off the floor (protraction).
3. **Hold:** Briefly pause at the peak of the movement.
4. **Return:** Lower the shoulder blade back to the floor in a controlled manner.

## Performance recommendations

- Frequency: 3-4 times per week
- Sets: 2-3
- Reps: 12-15 per arm
- Time: N/A
- Tempo: Controlled (2 sec up, 1 sec hold, 2 sec down)
- Range of motion: Maximum protraction of the scapula without lifting the torso.
- Pauses: 45-60 seconds between sets

## What to track

- pain before/after
- range of motion (how high the shoulder lifts)
- smoothness
- elbow bending (compensation)
- torso rotation (compensation)
- neck tension
- subjective difficulty

## Common errors

- **Bending the Elbow:** Using the triceps instead of the serratus anterior.
- **Rotating the Torso:** Rolling the body to lift the arm higher.
- **Neck Strain:** Tensing the neck or lifting the head off the floor.
- **Drifting Arm:** Letting the arm angle change; it should remain vertical.

## Restrictions

- Avoid if sharp pain occurs in the shoulder joint or ribs.
- Stop if unable to maintain a straight elbow.

## Conditions for adding to a cycle

- Minimum recovery stage: Early-stage (passive and early active motion allowed).
- Required equipment: Firm floor or treatment table.
- Whether protocol approval/prescription is required: Yes.
- Other conditions: Ability to lie supine and lift the arm to 90 degrees of flexion.
