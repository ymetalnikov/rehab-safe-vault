# AI Summary Format

## Purpose

The AI summary helps capture observations from a session or video, but doesn't replace medical advice.

### 5. Brief summary

1–3 sentences without confident medical conclusions.

## Forbidden format

Do not write:

- "you need to be treated this way";
- "you can definitely increase the load";
- "this is safe";
- confident conclusions from poor video.

## Good output format

> The video shows the exercise and the general trajectory, but the shoulder is partially obscured. The movement looks controlled, no noticeable jerks. Possible scapular compensation is hard to assess due to the angle. For comparing progress, it would be better to film from the side at the same camera distance next time.
