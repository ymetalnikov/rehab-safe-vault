# Progress Metrics

## What we track

### Regularity

- how many sessions occurred this week;
- which exercises were skipped;
- why they were skipped.

### Sensations

- pain before the exercise;
- pain after the exercise;
- stiffness;
- fatigue;
- subjective difficulty.

### Movement

- approximate range of motion;
- smoothness;
- control;
- possible compensation through torso/scapula/neck;
- similarity to the reference.

### Technique

- which errors were observed;
- whether they recur;
- whether compensations are decreasing;
- at what point in the movement the problem arises.

## How to view the trend

A single measurement isn't important on its own. Look at series:

- last 3 sessions;
- 7 days;
- 10 days;
- 30 days.

## Possible positive trend

- regularity holds;
- pain isn't rising;
- range gradually increases;
- movement is more controlled;
- fewer technique errors;
- the exercise feels subjectively easier.

## Possible negative trend

- pain rises;
- range drops;
- the same error recurs;
- the exercise becomes harder;
- post-session state is worse than usual.
