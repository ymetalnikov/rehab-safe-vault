# Tracker Guidelines

## What I add to the system

For each exercise:

- name;
- exercise goal;
- source/protocol;
- reference performance;
- key technique points;
- common errors;
- exactly what to track.

## What gets recorded after a session

- date;
- exercise;
- sets/reps/time;
- pain before and after;
- subjective sensation;
- approximate range of motion;
- technique notes;
- a brief summary.

## How we view progress

Not from a single session, but from a series:

- last 3 sessions;
- a week;
- 2 weeks;
- a month.

## What we consider a positive signal

- the exercise has become easier to perform;
- range of motion is trending up;
- pain isn't increasing;
- fewer technique errors;
- higher regularity;
- movement is becoming more controlled.

## What we consider a bad signal

- pain rises across several sessions in a row;
- range of motion drops;
- the same error keeps recurring;
- the exercise becomes harder;
- recovery after the session is worse than usual.
