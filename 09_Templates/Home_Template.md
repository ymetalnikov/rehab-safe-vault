## Project

Working title: **Rehab Safe Vault**

## Purpose

A personal recovery tracker: you define the exercise protocol and the reference performance, and the system helps record sessions, sensations, range of motion, technique errors, and the overall trend.

## Main scenario

1. Add exercises from your protocol to `05_Rehab_Protocols/Exercise_Database/`.
2. Describe ideal performance: video link, text, key technique cues.
3. Run a session — perform exercises on camera or log subjectively.
4. The system saves a session summary in `07_Session_Log/<date>/`.
5. Once a week, review the trend: regularity, pain, range of motion, technique.
