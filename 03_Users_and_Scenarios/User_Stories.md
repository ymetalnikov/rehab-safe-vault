# Main Scenario

## Me as the user

I'm recovering my shoulder and want to understand whether there's progress.

## What I do

1. I take an exercise from my protocol.
2. I add it to the database.
3. I attach a reference: video, link, or description.
4. I perform the exercise.
5. I record the result: pain, sensations, range of motion, technique.
6. I review the trend over a week or two.

## Why

- not to rely on memory;
- to see regularity;
- to notice recurring errors;
- to understand whether there's any dynamic;
- to collect questions and facts for myself or a specialist.

## Example user phrase

> Here's my protocol. Here's the exercise. Here's the ideal performance. Track my progress and show me what's changing over time.
