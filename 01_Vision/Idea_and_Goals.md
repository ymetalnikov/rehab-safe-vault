# Idea and Goals

## Problem

Shoulder recovery is slow, and without records it's hard to tell:

- whether there's real progress;
- which exercises work;
- where errors recur;
- when things got better or worse;
- how regularly I'm training.

Memory and feelings deceive. A journal is needed.

## Solution

A personal recovery tracker:

- store the exercise protocol;
- define a reference performance for each exercise;
- log every session;
- record pain, sensations, range of motion, and observations;
- review trends over a week / two weeks / a month.

## Goal

Make the recovery process visible and manageable at the level of a routine.

Not "what should I treat next," but:

- what I did;
- how often;
- what changed;
- where problems consistently arise;
- what questions I've accumulated for the specialist.

## Main hypothesis

If I regularly record the protocol, performance, and sensations, then in 2–4 weeks it becomes clearer whether there's progress and what's actually happening with recovery.
